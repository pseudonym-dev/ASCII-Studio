web/assets/examples/{toucan,macaw,chameleon}.bin.gz
  Pre-rendered ASCII grids of the three example clips (158 columns × 59 rows,
  12 fps, color, edge gain 1.10×, subject separation off). Packed and gzipped.
  Same authors and licenses as the source clips; they are adaptations of those
  videos. The committed grids were packed from 960 px intermediates of the same
  trims; the in-repo samples are 480 px so the single-file page stays under 3 MB.
  Rebuild from the in-repo clips with `node tools/pack-examples.mjs`.
